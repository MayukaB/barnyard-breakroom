Hen Pecks

How to play
  1. Today's answer is an animal-related expression. Each blank is one letter.
  2. You get 7 pecks. A peck is either one vowel (A E I O U Y) or one consonant pair, like BC or TZ. Every matching letter shows up.
  3. After your 7th peck you get a hint. Type the letters that are still missing.
  4. You have 3 tries. On a wrong try, the letters you got right stay put.
