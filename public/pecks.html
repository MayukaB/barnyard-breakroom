Hen Pecks

How to play
  1. Guess the animal-related expression. Each blank is one letter.
  2. You get 7 pecks. A peck is either one vowel (A E I O U Y) or one consonant pair, like BC or TZ. Every matching letter shows up in yellow.
  3. After your 7th peck you get a hint. You then have 3 tries to type the letters that are still missing. On a wrong try, the letters you got right show up in green.
  4. Hard mode keeps the hint hidden. You can still peek if you get stuck, but only a win without peeking counts as a hard-mode win. Switch it on or off before your 7th peck.
